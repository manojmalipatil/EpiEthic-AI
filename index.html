Under standard average-loss ERM with strict differential privacy, minority subgroups suffer disproportionate classification error. The Rawlsian model actively penalizes worst-off group loss to ensure equitable clinical detection.

Empirical Findings: Privacy × Fairness Interaction

01

Decoupled Orthogonal Axes

Differential Privacy is an informational constraint, not a competing distributive philosophy. A public health system can require both patient privacy and ethical fairness.

02

Bagdasaryan et al. (2019) Disparate Impact Finding

Under Average-Loss ERM, adding differential privacy noise (ε = 0.5) degrades accuracy on vulnerable socio-economic groups more severely than on the majority class.

03

Rawlsian Minimax Protection (Group DRO)

The Rawlsian objective (&min; &max;g ℒg) forces the optimization to place higher weights on the worst-off subgroup, effectively buffering it against privacy-induced degradation.
